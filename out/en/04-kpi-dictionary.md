# KPI dictionary — Dar Al-Khashab — Digital Expansion

Every indicator carries a definition, a formula, a target, a source, a cadence and an owner.

| Indicator | Area | Definition | Formula | Target | Measurement source | Cadence | Human owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Customer acquisition cost | Marketing | Advertising spend ÷ new customers | Spend ÷ new customers | ≤ EGP 380 | Meta + Google Ads | Weekly | Campaign Analyst Agent |

| Share of revenue from direct channels | Growth | Share of orders from the website, WhatsApp and mailing lists | Direct orders ÷ total orders | 40% by the end of month 12 | Order Hub | Weekly | Market Analyst Agent |

| Payment completion rate | Technology | Share of started payments that are completed | Successful payments ÷ started payments | ≥ 72% | Payment gateway | Daily | Integration Agent |
| First response time | Service | Time between a customer question and the first reply | Average time difference | < 5 minutes | WhatsApp API | Daily | Customer Service Agent |
