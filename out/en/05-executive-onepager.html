Summary

The project is financially viable in the base case, but the margin is thin: net present value is positive at only EGP 377,000, and the internal rate of return of 25.6% sits just 1.6 percentage points above the 24% discount rate - a gap that cannot honestly be called a comfortable margin. That finding is itself the most important output of the study, because it changes the nature of the decision: what is being requested is not a single unconditional approval, but a phased approval gated by measurement. The conservative scenario (price 10% lower, sales 25% slower, costs 15% higher) drives net present value to roughly EGP -11.6m and eliminates the internal rate of return altogether; the real risk therefore lies not in the market but in the conversion assumption. What supports the decision is that the operating risk is bounded: the production capacity exists, the workshop is running, and the genuine gap is the digital presence and the data - which is exactly what this transformation closes.

Recommendation

Approve the first phase with an EGP 8,500,000 budget split between immediate launch capital of EGP 5,700,000, and EGP 2,800,000 for the second CNC router held in escrow conditional upon achieving 70% capacity utilisation for three consecutive months. The 5-year cumulative capital expenditure (5-Year Cumulative CAPEX) totals EGP 10,270,000 (adding EGP 420,000 in Year 1 and EGP 1,350,000 in Year 2). The recommendation is conditional approval reflected in the five governance conditions.

Conditions

  1. Pass the 45-day marketing test at a conversion rate of at least 1.4% and an acquisition cost no higher than EGP 380 before releasing the second tranche.
  2. Sign supply agreements with at least two suppliers for every key material, with lead times no longer than 14 days.
  3. Appoint the digital project manager and the marketing manager within 30 days of the decision date.
  4. Complete a security review of the store before connecting the live payment gateway, and approve a tested backup plan.
  5. Do not release the production-line expansion tranche - second CNC router (EGP 2,800,000) held in escrow before current capacity utilisation reaches 70% for three consecutive months.
