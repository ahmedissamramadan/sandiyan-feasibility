    05
    Method

    The conceptual basis of feasibility

    Feasibility in its precise sense is not the question “does cost fall below return?” but the question “is the expectation built on enough evidence to make the risk acceptable?”

    Feasibility study versus appraisal

    Dimension of comparisonFeasibility studyTechnical/financial appraisal
    PurposeA decision to proceedMeasuring return on an existing investment
    Point in timeBefore the investmentAfter operation
    OutputA conditional recommendationA performance indicator
    Degree of certaintyHigh varianceLimited variance

    Certainty tiers used in this study

    • Recorded measures: Workshop operating records and actual sales, 2024–2026.
    • Measured: A survey of 240 respondents and 18 documented customer interviews.
    • Estimated: Total market size, category benchmark rates, and acquisition costs.
    • Stated assumptions: Discount rate, inflation and tax — editable in the live model.
